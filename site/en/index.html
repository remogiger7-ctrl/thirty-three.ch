Frequently asked questions

What does the apartment cost per month?

CHF 4'980 for the apartment and CHF 200 for the garage space, CHF 5'180 in total. Utilities are paid as a monthly advance (amount to follow) and settled once a year against actual costs.

When is the apartment available?

From 15 January or 1 February 2027, your choice. The building is completed in December 2026 – you are the first tenants.

How long is the lease?

Open-ended. Either side may terminate with three months' notice to the customary Zurich dates: end of March, June and September.

Which documents do I need?

None to enquire. If you would like the apartment after the viewing, you receive an application form; it includes a debt enforcement register extract – the Swiss certificate showing no outstanding debt proceedings.

What comes with the apartment?

Kitchen with all appliances, own washer and dryer, fibre-optic connection, electric blinds, storage room, cellar and garage space. The apartment is let unfurnished; it is a non-smoking apartment and subletting is not intended.
