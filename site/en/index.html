Rüschlikon on Lake Zurich

Live by the lake, work in the city: Rüschlikon lies on the western shore of Lake Zurich between Kilchberg and Thalwil, a few kilometres from Zurich. The lake is a ten-minute walk away, as are the station and the shops. Bus 165 runs into the city centre without changing, and from Rüschlikon station the S‑Bahn reaches Zurich main station in 15 minutes. And at 75 %, the municipal tax multiplier is one of the three lowest among the 160 municipalities in the canton of Zurich – the lower the multiplier, the lower your income tax.

Frequently asked questions

How does the deposit work?

The deposit of CHF 12'000 is paid into a blocked rental deposit account at a Swiss bank, held in your name and returned to you at the end of the lease. Alternatively, a rental deposit insurance is possible: you pay an annual premium instead of the lump sum.

What does “utilities on account” (Nebenkosten akonto) mean?

Utilities (typically heating, hot water and the building's running costs) are paid as a monthly advance and settled once a year against actual costs. The advance payment is CHF 300 per month.

What is a debt enforcement register extract (Betreibungsregisterauszug)?

A certificate from the debt enforcement office of your municipality confirming that no debt proceedings are pending against you. It is part of every rental application in Switzerland and is requested only after the viewing, not when you enquire.

Which notice periods apply?

The lease is open-ended. Notice can be given with three months to the end of March, June or September – the standard moving dates in the Zurich area.

Is the apartment step-free?

Yes. The lift stops directly inside the apartment; it can be reached without steps, with a pram or a wheelchair.
