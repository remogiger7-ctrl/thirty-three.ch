Utilities are paid as a monthly advance (Nebenkosten akonto) and settled once a year against actual costs; the amount is stated in the lease. The deposit is paid into a blocked rental deposit account at a Swiss bank, held in your name and returned to you at the end of the lease; alternatively, a rental deposit insurance (an annual premium instead of the lump sum) is possible. The notice period is three months, to the end of March, June or September – the standard moving dates in the Zurich area.

Frequently asked questions

What does the apartment cost per month?

CHF 4'980 for the apartment and CHF 200 for the garage space, CHF 5'180 in total. Utilities are paid as a monthly advance and settled once a year against actual costs; the amount is stated in the lease.

When is the apartment available?

From 15 January or 1 February 2027, your choice. The building will be completed in December 2026 – you will be the first tenants.

How long is the lease?

Open-ended. The notice period is three months, to the end of March, June or September – the standard moving dates in the Zurich area.

Which documents do I need?

None to enquire. If you would like the apartment after the viewing, you will receive an application form; it includes a debt enforcement register extract (Betreibungsregisterauszug) – a certificate from the local debt enforcement office confirming that no debt proceedings are pending against you.

What comes with the apartment?

Kitchen with all appliances, own washer and dryer, fibre-optic connection, electric blinds, storage room, cellar and garage space. The apartment is let unfurnished.
